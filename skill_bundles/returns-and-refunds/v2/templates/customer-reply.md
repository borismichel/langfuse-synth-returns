# Customer reply

Use this shape. Replace every brace, including the braces themselves.
Do not leave a placeholder in the text the customer reads.

Hello {customer_name},

{decision_sentence}

Amount: {refund_amount}
Policy: {policy_rule}

Next steps: {next_steps}

The decision sentence states the outcome in plain language (approved in full,
partly approved, store credit, declined, or passed to a colleague).
The amount line is the calculator's figure with a euro sign, or "none" when
the amount is null. The policy line is the rule id, such as R1-30-day-window.
Next steps say what the customer does now: a returns label, a store-credit
balance, a photo we still need, or nothing because the request was not a return.
